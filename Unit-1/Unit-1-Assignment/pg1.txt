1. Write a PL/SQL block to accept an employee's Basic Salary and
calculate:
• HRA = 20%
• DA = 45%
• TA = 8%
• PF = 12%
• Net Salary
Display all salary components along with Net Salary.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SET SERVEROUTPUT ON;
DECLARE
basic_salary NUMBER := &basic_salary;
hra NUMBER;
da NUMBER;
ta NUMBER;
pf NUMBER;
net_salary NUMBER;
BEGIN
hra := basic_salary * 0.20;
da := basic_salary * 0.45;
ta := basic_salary * 0.08;
pf := basic_salary * 0.12;
net_salary := basic_salary + hra + da + ta - pf;

DBMS_OUTPUT.PUT_LINE('Basic Salary : ' || basic_salary);
DBMS_OUTPUT.PUT_LINE('HRA : ' || hra);
DBMS_OUTPUT.PUT_LINE('DA : ' || da);
DBMS_OUTPUT.PUT_LINE('TA : ' || ta);
DBMS_OUTPUT.PUT_LINE('PF : ' || pf);

DBMS_OUTPUT.PUT_LINE('Net Salary : ' || net_salary);
END;
